# Product Context

Sadhox solves the problem of decentralized IT management by bringing Service Requests, Incident Management, Asset Management, and Change Approvals into a single unified platform. 

The primary users are:
- **End Users**: Requesting hardware, software, or IT support.
- **Support Agents**: Fulfilling requests and managing incidents.
- **Admins**: Managing assets, auditing logs, and overseeing system configuration.
